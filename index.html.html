Written Now, with a heavy heart
My love, my everything —

I don't know how to start this. I've rewritten it a hundred times and deleted every version because nothing feels like enough. But I know I have to say this, because silence is not something I can give you right now.

I hurt you. I know I did. And I hate myself for it more than you could ever know. The look on your face, the distance in your words — it cuts through me every single second. I caused that, and I own it completely.

✦

You deserve the truth, so here it is — everything I do, I do for you. Every early morning, every late night, every moment that looked suspicious or confusing from the outside — it was all coming from a place of love for you. I know that doesn't make it right. I know I should have been more open. I should have let you in instead of letting you wonder.

I was wrong to keep you guessing. You are not someone I need to hide anything from. You are the person I want to tell everything to. I just… got lost in my own head, and I didn't realize how it was making you feel. That's on me. Completely.

✦

I need you to know — once you truly see me, once you know me the way I want you to — you will never question me again. Not because I'm asking you to trust blindly, but because there is nothing hidden in my heart except love for you. No agenda. No lies. Just you.

I need you like the sky needs its stars. Without you, nothing makes sense. I breathe easier when I know you're okay. I smile before I even realize it when I think of you. You are not just someone in my life — you are the reason I want to be better every single day.

✦

Please don't give up on me. I'm not asking you to forget what happened. I'm asking you to give me the chance to show you who I really am — someone who loves you quietly, deeply, and without any reservation. Someone who made a mistake and is standing here, heart completely open, asking for one more chance.

I'm sorry. I'm so, so sorry. 💔
